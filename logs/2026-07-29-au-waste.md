Resolved via CKAN `package_show` by resource-name pattern with hard-coded fallback URLs.

## Architecture Decisions
- **Vanilla TS** (not React): one page, tab-switched views, no complex component tree.
- **Pipeline, not runtime-fetch:** the source is periodically published; the pipeline pre-aggregates into a compact cube (`waste.json`, ~58 KB) so the browser does no CSV parsing and derives every view from one source of truth.
- **Cube model:** the pipeline emits `[yearIdx, jurisIdx, catIdx, fateIdx, tonnes]` rows resolved through key arrays in `waste.json`, so the client's domain metadata can't silently drift from the cube indices.
- **Hand-rolled SVG** for the Sankey, treemap, bars, stacked area, heatmap and histogram (no chart library); Leaflet only for the map.
- **Deliberately not map-centric:** the story is material/fate/time, so the map is one of nine views, not the spine.

## Reconciliation gates
1. **Vocabulary/structural** — every row's material/jurisdiction/fate/management/core-flag must be in the known controlled vocabulary and Tonnes finite ≥ 0; a shifted column injects an unknown token and throws.
2. **Cross-file convergence** — the by-material and by-stream files are independently published breakdowns; in the stream-complete recent years (2019-20, 2020-21) their national totals must be EQUAL to within a tonne (verified reldiff 0.0). Earlier years legitimately diverge and are excluded.
Both pass exactly; the tests exercise both including deliberate-corruption failure cases.

## Errors & Resolutions
- **Preview launch resolved the wrong project.** A stale worktree-root `.claude/launch.json` ("foreign-owned") shadowed the site-level one; repointed it at au-waste's preview.
- **Mini-ranked bars had no coloured fill.** `.mr-fill` was an inline `<span>`, so `width:%` was ignored — added `display:block`. Found by zooming into the WA state drawer; fixed and re-verified.
- **`tsc` compiled the tests and choked on the untyped `.mjs`.** Scoped `tsconfig` `include` back to `["src"]`; vitest/esbuild type-checks the tests loosely and they pass.
- **jsdom lacks `svg.viewBox.baseVal`,** breaking `attachSvgZoom` in the render test. Added a viewBox-attribute fallback (harmless in browsers, makes the module headless-testable).
- **Two render-test isolation bugs** (stale detached drawers; a synthetic mover ordering) fixed in the tests, not the app.
