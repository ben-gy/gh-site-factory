## Idea Source
Researched. IDEAS.md was empty. Surveyed the 66-site catalog for a genuinely new domain and ruled out several candidates on data grounds: product/food recalls (data fragmented across state health CSVs, no clean national feed), Australian earthquakes (Geoscience Australia's API is an Angular SPA with no clean CORS JSON; USGS has CORS but poor intraplate AU coverage), and a power-station fleet map (OpenNEM now requires auth; the Geoscience Australia "Major Power Stations" open dataset is a static 2016 snapshot that undercuts the energy-transition story). Landed on **Births & Fertility** — a genuinely new domain for the catalog (nothing covers births, fertility or family formation; au-population is ERP/migration, au-mortality is deaths) with a clean, authoritative ABS SDMX source (the proven au-inflation/au-arrivals pipeline pattern) and a strong, highly-searchable story: a record-low fertility rate and the shift to much older motherhood.

## Data Sources
All from the ABS Data API (SDMX), CC BY 4.0:
- BIRTHS_SUMMARY — total births, crude birth rate, nuptial/ex-nuptial, population, by state, 1975–2024.
- FERTILITY_AGE_STATE — age-specific births/population/fertility rate by 5-year age group × state (→ TFR, ASFR).
- CONFINEMENTS_NUPTIALITY — median age of mother/father/first-time mother, median marriage duration at birth.
- BIRTHS_MONTH_OCCURRENCE — births by calendar month of occurrence × state (seasonality).
- ABS ASGS 2021 state boundaries (vendored `patterns/geo/au-states.geojson`).

## Key Facts (verified live)
- TFR 2.148 (1975) → **1.481 (2024)**, a record low, below the 2.1 replacement rate since 1976.
- Median age of mother 25.8 → 32.1 (+6.3y); teen fertility 40.1 → 6.0 per 1,000 (−85%); 35–39 (66.6) now > 20–24 (30.6).
- 39% of births ex-nuptial (was 10% in 1975). NT highest TFR (1.629), ACT lowest (1.273).

## Architecture Decisions
- Vanilla TS (single-page tabbed explorer, no heavy component tree). Leaflet only for the choropleth; every other chart hand-rolled SVG (line, bars, stacked area, heatmap, histogram) driven by pure layout maths in `src/layout.ts` (the positional-test target). Copied canonical patterns `tooltip.ts` and `svgZoom.ts`; did not need treemap/network for this data shape.
- Three reconciliation gates in the dependency-free `pipeline/parse.mjs` (shared with tests): (1) cross-file EXACT — the two independent "all births" columns agree (0.000%); (2) TFR = 5·Σ(ASFR)/1000 tolerant (1.14%); (3) nuptial+ex-nuptial = total (3.96%, the Tasmanian not-stated residual). Gate 1 was redesigned mid-build after discovering the age-group sum legitimately falls short of the total by up to 7.5% (maternal age not stated) — so it compares published all-ages totals, not the group sum.

## Errors & Resolutions
1. **Gate 1 mis-designed** — initially summed the 5-year age-group births and compared to the summary total; failed at up to 7.5% for small states because maternal-age-not-stated births are excluded from the age breakdown. Redesigned to compare the two independently published *all-ages* totals (exact), and exposed the not-stated share as a non-gated info metric.
2. **Seasonality incompleteness** — the latest data year (2024) is incomplete by month of occurrence (Dec = 0.18× the median month) because of registration lag. An initial completeness heuristic (Σmonths vs the registration total) wrongly flagged many old years, because occurrence and registration are different universes (they differ by up to 5%). Switched to an internal test — a year is complete iff its smallest month ≥ 50% of that year's median — which flags only 2024.
3. **Drawer/modal never opened in a backgrounded tab** — the open transition was triggered via `requestAnimationFrame`, which browsers throttle when the tab isn't focused, so the `.open` class never applied. Switched to a forced reflow (`void el.offsetWidth`), which is robust.
4. **Cold deep-link to #map showed a world-wrapped map** — a single fit ran before Leaflet's projection settled. Added multi-delay + `whenReady` + ResizeObserver fit retries that stop once the boundary bounds sit inside the viewport.
5. **375px overflow on the Older-mothers view** — the 12-button year selector didn't wrap; added `flex-wrap`.
6. **Stale hover-tooltip after a nav switch** — cleared the `.hover-tip` on navigate.
